      How it works

      Same seed, same question: the team's saved answer is shown. The model is not asked again.

      New seed or new question: the model is asked once and the answer is saved for the team, with your email and the time.

      A team is either open or closed. An open team can be used by anyone with this link. A closed team needs a sign-in and membership. Either way, the page never talks to the database or the model: a service checks every request first.

      The model still answers differently every time. The seed is what makes one answer repeatable. A changed word counts as a new question.
